01 — Background

Two kinds of client, one way of working

I got into engineering through the parts that break in production, not the ones that demo well. At Pure Billion Technologies, that meant migrating media storage to a hardened AWS S3 setup, then benchmarking Gemini, GPT, and Claude against each other until one of them earned its place in the pipeline on cost, speed, and accuracy — not just novelty.

Freelancing for Shah International meant something more hands-on: a WordPress theme built without a page builder, an AJAX filter system fast enough that nobody notices it's there, and a full security audit before launch, because a laminate distributor's product catalog is still someone's livelihood.

Outside client work, I've published research on operating-systems foundations for autonomous vehicles and on detecting AI-generated deepfakes. The thread running through all of it is the same: I want to know how a system actually fails before I trust it to run.
